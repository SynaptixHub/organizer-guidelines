# 📖 Hack Trick Organizer Guidelines

Welcome to the **Hack Trick Organizing Team**! By joining the team, you become a representative of both **Hack Trick** and **Synaptix Hub**. We expect every organizer to uphold the highest standards of professionalism, teamwork, and integrity.

## 1. Professionalism

* Treat every participant, mentor, sponsor, speaker, volunteer, and fellow organizer with respect.
* Be punctual for meetings, deadlines, and event activities.
* Communicate professionally in all official channels.
* Represent Hack Trick and Synaptix Hub positively at all times.

## 2. Commitment

* Complete assigned tasks before the agreed deadlines.
* Inform your team lead as early as possible if you are unable to complete a task or attend a meeting.
* Stay actively involved throughout the planning and execution of the event.

## 3. Communication

* Regularly check official communication platforms (WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, Email, etc.).
* Respond to important messages within a reasonable timeframe.
* Keep your team informed about your progress and any challenges.

## 4. Teamwork

* Collaborate respectfully with all teams and departments.
* Be open to feedback and willing to help others.
* Focus on solving problems together rather than assigning blame.

## 5. Confidentiality

As an organizer, you may have access to confidential information such as participant data, sponsor discussions, financial information, internal documents, and future plans.

Do not:

* Share confidential information outside the organizing team.
* Leak internal documents, meeting recordings, or planning discussions.
* Share participant or organizer personal information without authorization.

## 6. Respect & Inclusion

Hack Trick is committed to creating a welcoming environment for everyone.

Discrimination, harassment, bullying, hate speech, intimidation, or any form of inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated.

Respect differences in background, gender, religion, culture, nationality, and experience.

## 7. Social Media

* Do not make official announcements unless authorized.
* Avoid sharing confidential information or behind-the-scenes discussions publicly.
* When posting about Hack Trick, maintain professionalism and respect the brand.

## 8. Conflict of Interest

Inform your team lead if you have any personal, financial, or professional relationship that may influence decisions related to sponsors, judges, vendors, or participants.

## 9. Event Responsibility

During the event:

* Be approachable and helpful.
* Assist participants whenever possible.
* Follow instructions from department leads and the core organizing team.
* Handle unexpected situations calmly and professionally.

## 10. Use of Community Resources

Community accounts, documents, software, branding assets, and communication channels are to be used only for official Hack Trick and Synaptix Hub activities.

Unauthorized access, misuse, or sharing of these resources is strictly prohibited.

## 11. Privacy

Organizer and participant information is collected solely for official community purposes.

Any use of personal information or photographs beyond official operations will only take place after obtaining the individual's permission.

## 12. Code of Conduct

Every organizer is expected to:

* Act honestly and ethically.
* Respect everyone regardless of their role.
* Follow the instructions of the Core Team.
* Maintain a positive and collaborative attitude.
* Help create a safe, welcoming, and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## 13. Consequences of Misconduct

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in:

* Verbal or written warning
* Removal from specific responsibilities
* Suspension from the organizing team
* Permanent removal from Hack Trick and Synaptix Hub activities, depending on the severity of the violation

## Acknowledgement

By joining the Hack Trick Organizing Team, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to follow these Organizer Guidelines. Together, we can create a professional, inclusive, and memorable experience for everyone involved.
