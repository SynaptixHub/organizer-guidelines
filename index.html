Hack Trick Organizer Guidelines

BUILD • COLLABORATE • LEAD • INSPIRE

👋 Welcome

Welcome to the Hack-Trick Hackathon Organizing Team! By joining this team, you become a representative of both Hack-Trick Hackathon These guidelines help us maintain a professional, collaborative and respectful environment while delivering an unforgettable hackathon experience.

💼 Professionalism

  • Treat every participant, mentor, sponsor, speaker, volunteer, and organizer with respect.
  • Be punctual for meetings, deadlines, and event activities.
  • Communicate professionally across all official platforms.
  • Represent Hack Trick and Synaptix Hub positively at all times.

🚀 Commitment

  • Complete assigned tasks before deadlines.
  • Inform your Team Lead immediately if you cannot attend a meeting or complete work.
  • Remain active throughout planning and event execution.

📢 Communication

  • Regularly check WhatsApp, Discord, Slack and Email.
  • Respond to important messages promptly.
  • Keep your team updated regarding progress and blockers.

🤝 Teamwork

  • Work respectfully with every department.
  • Accept constructive feedback.
  • Support fellow organizers whenever possible.
  • Solve problems together rather than assigning blame.

🔒 Confidentiality

As an organizer you may have access to confidential information including participant data, sponsor discussions, financial information and planning documents.


  • Never share confidential information outside the organizing team.
  • Do not leak internal meetings or documents.
  • Do not share personal information without authorization.

🌍 Respect & Inclusion

  • Create a welcoming environment for everyone.
  • No discrimination, bullying, harassment or hate speech.
  • Respect differences in gender, culture, religion, nationality and experience.

📱 Social Media

  • Only authorized members may publish official announcements.
  • Never disclose confidential discussions publicly.
  • Maintain Hack Trick's professional image.

⚖ Conflict of Interest

Inform your Team Lead if any personal, financial or professional relationship could influence decisions involving sponsors, judges, vendors or participants.

🎯 Event Responsibilities

  • Be approachable and helpful.
  • Support participants whenever needed.
  • Follow instructions from department leads.
  • Handle unexpected situations calmly and professionally.

🖥 Community Resources

Community accounts, branding assets, software, documents and communication channels are for official Hack Trick and Synaptix Hub activities only. Unauthorized use or sharing is prohibited.

🔐 Privacy

Organizer and participant information is collected only for official community operations. Photos or personal information will never be used outside community activities without obtaining prior permission.

✅ Organizer Code of Conduct

  • Act honestly and ethically.
  • Respect everyone equally.
  • Follow Core Team instructions.
  • Maintain a positive attitude.
  • Create a safe, inclusive and memorable event experience.

🚨 Consequences of Misconduct

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Removal from responsibilities
  • Suspension from the organizing team
  • Permanent removal from Hack Trick or Synaptix Hub activities

📜 Acknowledgement

By joining the Hack Trick Organizing Team, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to follow these Organizer Guidelines. Together, we can build a professional, innovative and welcoming hackathon experience for everyone.
